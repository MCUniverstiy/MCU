# 📨 Contact Enquiries Management Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/enquiries`

This guide explains how to read, track, and manage incoming questions submitted through the website's **Contact Us** form.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
Whenever a visitor or prospective corporate partner fills out the contact form on `/contact` or requests custom corporate training, their message lands directly in the **Enquiry Manager**.

---

## 📥 How to Review & Respond to Enquiries

1. Navigate to **Enquiry Manager** (`/admin/enquiries`).
2. You will see a chronological list of incoming messages, with the newest enquiries at the top.
3. Each message card displays:
   * **Subject Line**
   * **Sender's Name**
   * **Email Address** *(Clickable `mailto:` link to reply directly from your email app)*
   * **Phone Number** (if provided)
   * **Full Message Text**
   * **Submission Date & Time**

---

## 🏷️ How to Update Status Tracking

To help your team stay organized, each enquiry has a status dropdown menu in the top-right corner of the message card:

* **New**: Fresh enquiry that hasn't been reviewed yet.
* **In progress**: Team member is currently following up with the customer.
* **Resolved**: Customer has been answered and their enquiry is completed.

Simply select the new status from the dropdown — it saves automatically!

---

## 💡 Workflow Tip for Team Communication

* When you start working on an enquiry, change its status from **New** to **In progress** so other team members know it's being handled.
* Once you reply to the customer via email or phone, change its status to **Resolved**.
