# 📦 Shop Orders Management Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/orders`

This guide explains how to track, review, and fulfill customer shop orders for merchandise and publications.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
Whenever a customer buys items from the MCU Shop (`/shop`), an order is generated here with item details, delivery address, notes, and total price.

---

## 📋 How to Review Orders

1. Navigate to **Order Manager** (`/admin/orders`).
2. Each order card shows:
   * **Order Number & Customer Name** (e.g. `Order #12 — John Smith`)
   * **Customer Email & Phone Number**
   * **Order Total Amount (HK$)**
   * **Delivery / Shipping Address**
   * **Itemized List** of purchased products and quantities (e.g. `Financial Planning Handbook × 2 (HK$250)`)
   * **Order Date & Timestamp**

---

## 🚚 How to Update Order Statuses

Use the dropdown menu next to each order to update its progress:

| Status | Meaning & Next Step |
| :--- | :--- |
| **New** | Order just received. Check items and prepare for packaging. |
| **Confirmed** | Payment verified; item is being picked from inventory. |
| **Processing** | Order is being packed and prepared for shipment. |
| **Shipped** | Package has been handed over to the courier / delivery service. |
| **Completed** | Customer received their order successfully. |
| **Cancelled** | Order was refunded or cancelled. |

Simply select the status from the dropdown menu — changes save instantly!
