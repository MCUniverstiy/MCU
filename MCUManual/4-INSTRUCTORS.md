# 👨‍🏫 Instructors Management Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/cms/instructors`

This guide explains how to manage faculty profiles, instructor bios, specializations, and profile photos.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
Instructors created here can be assigned to courses on the **Courses** page (`/admin/cms/courses`). Instructor names and photos appear on public course cards and course detail pages so students know who is teaching.

---

## ➕ How to Add a New Instructor

1. Navigate to **Content Manager → Instructors** (`/admin/cms/instructors`).
2. Click **`+ Add instructor`**.
3. Fill out the instructor fields in the editor panel:
   * **First Name \***: e.g. `David`
   * **Last Name \***: e.g. `Chan`
   * **Specialization / Title**: e.g. `Senior Fellow, Family Office Practice`
   * **Biography**: Background, credentials, experience, and academic background.
   * **Photo**: Upload a professional headshot photo.
4. Click **Save instructor**.

---

## 🔗 How to Assign an Instructor to a Course

1. Go to **Content Manager → Courses** (`/admin/cms/courses`).
2. Click **Edit** next to any course.
3. Locate the **Instructor** dropdown menu.
4. Select the instructor's name from the list.
5. Click **Save course**.

---

## 💡 Best Practices for Interns

* **Headshot Quality:** Ensure headshots are clear, professional, well-lit, and square/portrait-cropped.
* **Consistent Titles:** Use official academic or professional designations (e.g. `Prof.`, `Dr.`, or `Managing Director`).
