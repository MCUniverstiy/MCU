# 👥 Student Directory & CRM Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/students`

This guide explains how to view student profiles, track course enrollments, check membership status, and print official completion certificates.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
The Student Directory acts as your central CRM (Customer Relationship Management) database. It gives administrators a 360-degree view of every registered student, their membership level, course history, total amount spent, and earned certificates.

---

## 🔍 How to Search and Filter Students

1. Navigate to **Student Management** (`/admin/students`).
2. Use the search bar and filter pills at the top:
   * **Search Bar**: Search by student name, email address, Student Number, or Member ID.
   * **Filter Pills**:
     * **All**: Shows all registered students.
     * **💳 Members Only**: Filters to show only active VIP members.
     * **Non-Members**: Filters to show non-member students.

---

## 📊 Overview Table Columns

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Student ID / Member ID** | Unique student reference number (e.g. `MCU-2026-0041`) and VIP Member ID. |
| **Name** | Student's full name. |
| **Email** | Contact email address. |
| **Tier** | Active membership tier (e.g. `International Member`). |
| **Courses** | Total number of enrolled courses. |
| **Certificates** | Number of completion certificates earned. |
| **Total Paid** | Total revenue generated from this student across all paid courses. |

---

## 🔽 Expanding a Student Record (Detailed View)

Click anywhere on a student's row to expand their full record!

When expanded, you can see:
1. **Student Contact & Membership Details**: Phone number, area of interest, registration date, and membership discount percentage.
2. **Course Enrollment History**: Table showing every course the student enrolled in, enrollment date, payment status (`Paid` or `Pending`), course grade (`Pass` or `Fail`), and certificate number.

---

## 🎓 How to View & Print a Student's Certificate

1. Expand the student's row by clicking on it.
2. Under **Enrollments & Certificates**, locate the course with an issued certificate.
3. Click the gold **`🎓 View Certificate`** button.
4. An official digital certificate modal will open on screen.
5. Click **`🖨 Print / Save as PDF`** to print a hard copy or save a PDF file to send to the student!
