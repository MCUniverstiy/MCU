# ✅ Grading Dashboard & Certificates Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/grades`

This guide explains how to select courses, review enrolled students, assign Pass/Fail course grades, and issue digital certificates.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
The Grading Dashboard allows instructors and administrators to grade students upon course completion. Marking a student as **Pass** automatically generates an official certificate of completion with a unique certificate number (e.g. `MCU-2026-000104`).

---

## 📝 How to Grade Students (Step-by-Step)

1. Navigate to **Grading Dashboard** (`/admin/grades`).
2. At the top of the page, select the course you want to grade from the **Course** dropdown selector (e.g., `Course 1 — CEO Wealth Management Masterclass`).
3. The student enrollment table will load for that selected course.
4. For each student listed in the table, click one of the action buttons under **Actions**:

| Action Button | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`Pass`** (Green) | Marks the student as **Pass** and **automatically generates their certificate**! |
| **`Fail`** (Red) | Marks the student as **Fail**. No certificate is generated. |
| **`Clear`** (Grey) | Clears the grade back to **Ungraded**. |

---

## ⚡ Bulk Grading ("Mark All Ungraded as Pass")

If an entire cohort completes a course successfully:
1. Select the course from the dropdown.
2. Click the green **`Mark All Ungraded as Pass`** button at the top.
3. Every student currently marked as *Ungraded* will instantly be set to *Pass*, and official certificates will be issued for all of them at once!

---

## 🎨 Customizing the Certificate Design (`/admin/template`)

Administrators can customize the design, background image, font positions, and text layout for all issued certificates:

1. Navigate to **Certificate Template Manager** (`/admin/template`).
2. You can upload a custom certificate background image (PNG or JPG).
3. Adjust font sizes, margins, line heights, and coordinates for the 4 auto-filled fields:
   * **Recipient Name**
   * **Course Title**
   * **Certificate Number**
   * **Issue Date**
4. Click **Save Template**. All future printed certificates will automatically use your updated design!
