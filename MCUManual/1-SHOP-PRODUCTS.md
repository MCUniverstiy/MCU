# 🛍️ Shop Products Management Guide

**URL:** `https://your-mcu-site.com/admin/cms/products`

This guide explains how to manage shop merchandise, publications, digital products, and items sold in the MCU Institute Shop.

---

## 🎯 What This Section Does
Items created here appear on the public **Shop** page (`/shop`). Customers can view products, add them to their shopping cart, and check out with Stripe.

---

## ➕ How to Add a New Product

1. Go to **Content Manager → Shop Products** (`/admin/cms/products`).
2. Click the red **`+ Add product`** button at the top of the page.
3. The **Add item** form panel will open on the right side of your screen.
4. Fill out the fields:
   * **Title \***: Name of the product (e.g., `Financial Planning Handbook 2026`).
   * **Category**: Item category (e.g., `Books`, `Merchandise`, `Digital`).
   * **Price \***: Price of the product (e.g., `HK$250` or `250`).
   * **Description**: A short summary explaining what the product is, what's included, or sizing information.
   * **Photo**: Click **Choose File** / **Browse** to upload an image of the item.
5. Click **Save**.
6. The product will immediately appear on the public Shop page!

---

## ✏️ How to Edit or Delete an Existing Product

1. Find the product in the list on the left.
2. Click the **Edit** button next to the product:
   * Update the title, price, category, description, or photo.
   * Click **Save** to publish your changes.
3. **To delete a product:** Click the red **Delete** button next to the product and confirm when prompted.

---

## 💡 Best Practices for Interns

* **High-Quality Photos:** Use clean product photos with bright lighting and clear backgrounds.
* **Clear Categories:** Keep category names consistent (e.g., use `Books` instead of mixing `Book`, `Books`, and `Textbooks`).
* **Double Check Prices:** Ensure prices match your official catalog before clicking Save.
